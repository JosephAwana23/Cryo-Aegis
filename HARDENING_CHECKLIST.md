# 📋 1-Page European Cold-Chain & Clinical Hardening Checklist

> **Target Audience:** Hospital Biomedical Engineering Teams, Blood Bank Directors, & Supermarket Facility Managers  
> **Purpose:** Immediate, actionable steps to secure unpatched OT refrigeration controllers (`CVE-2025-41450`, `CVE-2025-41451`, `ONEDAY` credential abuse) against remote thermal sabotage and "Denial of View" attacks.

---

## 1. Eliminate "Shadow-4G" Public Internet Exposure
* **[ ] Audit Cellular Gateways:** Verify whether a standalone 4G/5G cellular SIM modem or router is physically attached directly to your Danfoss, Copeland, or Carel refrigeration controller for remote vendor maintenance.
* **[ ] Disable Port Forwarding:** Ensure ports `80`, `443`, `502` (Modbus/TCP), and `47808` (BACnet) are **never** exposed directly to the public internet or Censys/Shodan search engines.
* **[ ] Enforce VPN & MFA:** Force all third-party HVAC and refrigeration maintenance contractors to connect strictly through an encrypted VPN (such as WireGuard or IPsec) protected by Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) before reaching the OT VLAN.

## 2. Patch Firmware & Lock Down Default Maintenance Accounts
* **[ ] Patch Danfoss Controllers:** Immediately upgrade all Danfoss `AK-SM 800A` (StoreView) controllers to **Firmware v4.2 or higher** to permanently remediate `CVE-2025-41450` (Authentication Bypass), `CVE-2025-41451` (Command Injection), and `CVE-2025-41452` (Nginx Config Injection).
* **[ ] Disable Predictable Logins:** Disable the daily maintenance `ONEDAY` (Code-of-the-Day) account on Copeland `XWEB` / `E3` and Danfoss systems. Rotate all default `admin` credentials to strong, unique passphrases.

## 3. Deploy Independent "Out-of-Band" Telemetry & Hardware Failsafes
* **[ ] Separate Control from Alerting:** As demonstrated in successful EFS blood bank incident responses, never rely solely on the vendor's management console for both temperature control and high-temperature alarms. Install an independent, out-of-band monitoring system (such as **Cryo-Aegis**) or a read-only thermocouple probe.
* **[ ] Verify Physical Thermal Cutouts:** Ensure every walk-in dairy chiller and clinical plasma freezer possesses a physical, non-networked bimetallic thermal cutout switch or hardwired relay that automatically cuts electrical power to defrost heating elements if internal temperatures exceed safe clinical thresholds (e.g., above +6°C for blood/perishables).
